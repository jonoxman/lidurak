## Requirements to Verify & Remediate

### 1. Consistent `'10'` Rank Typography, Suit Sizing, & Overlap Clearance (Item #2)
- Ensure the `'10'` rank glyph on playing cards renders at the **exact same font size** as all other numbered ranks (`2`–`9`), removing any smaller font override for `'10'`.
- Increase the side/corner suit indicator size so it makes full use of the widened corner/strip needed for `'10'`.
- Update the maximal allowable card overlap (in the active player's hand and any other overlapped card displays) so that the full two-digit `'10'` rank and enlarged suit indicator remain completely visible whenever overlap is at or below the maximal allowable threshold.

### 2. Large Hand Layout Rules — Fixed Card Size & Option B Box Progression (Item #5)
- **Never shrink card dimensions** (`width` / `height`) for large hands; cards in hand must stay full-sized matching the Row 2 throw-in/redirect outline dimensions.
- Enforce the **Option B** width/overlap progression across two container bounds:
  1. **Default 6-Card Box:** Width equals the standard 6-card hand width (including overlap). Small/normal hands (≤ 6 cards) stay within this default box without shrinking cards.
  2. **Maximal Box:** Almost the full width of the play area with small symmetric margins on both left and right sides, with zero overflow past the viewport or into `[data-zone="table-deck"]`, `[data-zone="sidebar-left"]`, `[data-zone="sidebar-right"]`, or action controls.
  3. **Progression (Option B):** As card count increases beyond the default 6-card box (e.g., 14 cards in `qa-medium-14-expand`), the hand expands outward toward the maximal box while maintaining full rank/suit visibility (up to the Item #2 maximal allowable overlap). Once the hand at maximal allowable overlap would exceed the maximal box width (e.g., 30 cards in `qa-large-30-compress`), increase overlap **beyond** the maximal allowable amount (compressing further so rank/suit need not remain fully visible) so all cards remain inside the maximal box at full size.
- Ensure the responsive non-rectangular hand highlight contour tightly wraps the actual rendered card positions across all hand sizes and lift tiers.

### 3. Smooth Hover "Parting" Animation (Item #6)
- Mousing over cards in `[data-zone="player-hand"]` must smoothly animate "parting" the hand: reducing overlap for cards close to the mouse pointer while increasing overlap for cards farther away so the hand remains cleanly bounded.
- Use smooth, aesthetically clean CSS transitions / interpolated offsets.
- **Single-Card Guard:** When `hand.length <= 1` (e.g., `qa-single-card`), the parting animation MUST NOT run and card position must not shift.
- **Stationary Hovered Card:** The card currently being moused over MUST NOT change its horizontal position due to the parting animation.

### 4. Two-Tier Vertical Card Lift (Item #8)
- Replace the single vertical lift offset with two distinct vertical lift tiers in `[data-zone="player-hand"]`:
  - **Baseline (0):** Unplayable / ineligible cards.
  - **Tier 1 (Halfway):** Cards that are legally eligible to be played are raised **halfway** to the full selected height.
  - **Tier 2 (Full):** Cards actively selected by the user are raised **fully** to the full height.
- Ensure the non-rectangular hand highlight contour accurately tracks all three vertical levels (baseline, halfway eligible, full selected).

### 5. Zero-Offset Dragging Bug Fix (Item #3)
- Fix any coordinate/origin calculation causing a dragged card to drift horizontally by an offset that grows the farther right the card sits in the hand (accounting for negative margins, CSS transforms, parting offsets, scroll/viewport coordinates, and grab-point anchor).
- The dragged card must track the mouse pointer with **zero horizontal or vertical offset** from every index in the hand (leftmost, middle, rightmost, in both 6-card and 30-card hands) and remain visible (`opacity !== 0`, `visibility: visible`) throughout the drag lifecycle.
