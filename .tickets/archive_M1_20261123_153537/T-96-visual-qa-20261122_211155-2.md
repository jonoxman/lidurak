# Arm `qa-surrender-timer` Preset with Short Active Countdown so Surrender Pickup Resolves

## Problem Description
Real Chromium telemetry (`step_9_item_4_surrender_initial.png`, `step_10_item_4_after_surrender.png`) reported two failures on Item 4:
- `Item 4 step 4: Expected .table-pair to clear after surrender pickup (remaining=4)`
- `Item 4 step 4: Expected hand count to increase by 5 (before=6, after=6)`

In `backend/src/main/scala/durak/server/GameManager.scala` (`qaSurrenderTimerPreset`, lines 533–581), `qa-surrender-timer` starts already in `BoutPhase.SurrenderThrowIn` (so Alice, `p0`, has `legalActions = []` and no `Surrender` button is rendered) while Bob (`p1`) and Carol (`p2`) hold matching Jacks (`J♠`, `J♥`, `J♦`), keeping `promptedPlayerIds` non-empty. However, `GameActor.scala` (`createRoom` / `startRoom`, line 148) initializes `turnDeadlineMs` to `now + turnSeconds * 1000L` (`15` seconds). Because `15` seconds is longer than the browser QA step wait (only 1 second elapsed between `step_9` and `step_10`, ticking Bob's clock from `00:15` to `00:14`), the surrender throw-in clock does not expire during verification, so `.table-pair` never clears and the 5 surrendered table cards (`10♣`, `J♣`, `10♠`, `10♦`, `10♥`) never transfer into Alice's hand.

## Required Changes
1. **`backend/src/main/scala/durak/server/GameManager.scala` & `backend/src/main/scala/durak/server/GameActor.scala`:**
   - Support a short initial turn-clock duration (e.g., `3` seconds) for `qa-surrender-timer` (either via a `turnSecondsOverride` / `initialTurnSeconds` on `PresetGame` or initializing `qa-surrender-timer`'s room with `turnSeconds = 3` or a `3s` initial `turnDeadlineMs` when `state.turnDeadlineMs > 0L` is pre-populated / overridden by the preset).
   - Keep all existing `PresetSuite` invariants intact: `defaultPlayerId = "p0"`, `boutPhase = BoutPhase.SurrenderThrowIn`, `attackerIdx = 2`, `defenderIdx = 0`, `tablePairs.size = 4` (1 covered, 3 uncovered), `promptedPlayerIds.toSet == Set("p1", "p2")`, `turnPlayerId == Some("p1")`, `turnDeadlineMs > 0L`, `turnSecondsRemaining > 0` (now `<= 3`), and `bob`/`carol` holding their throw-in Jacks.
   - Ensure when the 3-second countdown reaches `00:00`, `GameActor.autoPlay` passes Bob and Carol via `autoActionsFor`, resolving the surrendered bout (`resolveBout`), clearing `tablePairs`, and transferring the 5 table cards into Alice's hand (`6 -> 11` cards) cleanly.
