# Audit Gap Verification & Remediation: Curated 4-Preset QA Scenario Suite End-to-End Wiring (Item #9)

## Context
During the final pre-release audit on `main`, `GameManager.scala` was truncated before `qaLarge30CompressPreset` and `qaSingleCardPreset`, and all frontend pages (`LobbyPage.tsx`, `GamePage.tsx`) were omitted from the audit payload. Verify end-to-end alignment of the reduced QA Scenario Preset set across backend and frontend without overwriting working code.

## Requirements to Verify & Remediate
1. **Backend Curated Presets (`GameManager.scala`):**
   - Confirm `GameManager.Presets` contains only the reduced set of current-round QA scenarios:
     - `qa-normal-6-actions` ("6-Card 10s & Actions")
     - `qa-medium-14-expand` ("14-Card Expand Box")
     - `qa-large-30-compress` ("30-Card Compress & Part")
     - `qa-single-card` ("1-Card Hand (No Part)")
   - Verify `qaLarge30CompressPreset` and `qaSingleCardPreset` preserve exact card-conservation invariants (no duplicate/missing cards), valid `bottomTrumpCard`, and expose the intended states:
     - `qa-large-30-compress`: 30-card defending hand with mixed playable/unplayable cards, `"cover"` and `"surrender"` legal actions (verifying Items #2, #3, #4, #5 Option B compression, #6 parting, #7 Surrender button, #8 two-tier raise).
     - `qa-single-card`: 1-card active hand holding `10♠` (verifying Item #2 `'10'` rank & Item #6 1-card no-parting guard).
   - Confirm all obsolete presets from previous rounds are removed from `GameManager.Presets` and `GameManager.preset(...)`.
2. **Frontend Preset Wiring (`LobbyPage.tsx`, `GamePage.tsx`):**
   - Confirm the Lobby and `[data-zone="sidebar-left"]` QA Scenario Preset triggers expose **only** the reduced current-round presets (`qa-normal-6-actions`, `qa-medium-14-expand`, `qa-large-30-compress`, `qa-single-card`) with clear labels and zero stale/deleted preset IDs left in the UI.
   - Verify clicking each preset button calls `POST /api/games/preset` with the exact backend preset ID and loads the game cleanly.
