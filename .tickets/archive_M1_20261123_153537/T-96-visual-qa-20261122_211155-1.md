# Fix Small-Hand Hover-Parting Grid Shift, Smoothness, and POV Tab Switching

## Problem Description
Real Chromium telemetry and screenshot inspection (`step_11_item_6_6card_hand_rest.png`, `step_12_item_6_6card_hand_parted.png`, `step_4_item_1_left_edge_pov.png`) identified two objective frontend bugs:

1. **Small-hand hover parting shifts the entire row left instead of parting outward around a stationary hovered card (Item 6):**
   - Telemetry error: `Item 6 step 4: Card 3 should shift right on hover of card 2 (rest=626.0, settled=624.0)`.
   - In `frontend/src/styles/table.css` (`.hand-cards`, lines 1233–1264), `.hand-cards` uses `display: grid; grid-template-columns: auto; justify-content: center;` with an inline `width` equal to `handLayoutState.totalWidth` (`420px` for a 6-card hand in `qa-normal-6-actions`).
   - Inside `.hand-cards`, `<svg class="hand-highlight-contour">` (`grid-area: 1 / 1`) receives inline `marginLeft: ${handContour.left}px` (`-4px`) and `width: ${handContour.width}px`. At rest, its margin-box contribution is `428 - 4 = 424px`, shifting the `auto` track by `(420 - 424) / 2 = -2px` (placing card 3 at `626px` instead of `628px`). When hovering card 2 (`10♣`), `computePartedCardLayout` expands the right-side cards by `+33.3px`, growing `handContour.width` to `461.3px` (`457.3px` margin-box width). Because `.hand-cards` has `grid-template-columns: auto; justify-content: center;`, the widened `auto` track centers inside the `420px` box by `(420 - 457.3) / 2 = -18.65px` (`-17px` relative to rest), shifting the entire `.hand-row` (`grid-area: 1 / 1; justify-items: start`) left by `-17px` with zero transition.
   - As a result, the hovered card (card 2) moved from `562px` to `545px` instead of staying pinned, and card 3 moved from `626px` to `624px` (`+14.67 - 17 = -2.33px`) instead of shifting right, while causing an untweened horizontal jump on hover.

2. **Left-sidebar POV switching race & tab semantics (Item 1 step 8):**
   - Telemetry error: `Item 1 step 8: Expected covered pairs to render with data-covered-edge="left" for top-left/top-center/bottom defender POV`.
   - In `frontend/src/pages/GamePage.tsx` (lines 474–506), switching `playerId` within the same `gameId` (`viewGameRef.current === gameId`) does not clear `view`, and line 505 runs `setView((current) => current ?? initial)`. Because `current` is still the previous player's non-null `PlayerGameView`, `current ?? initial` discards the freshly fetched `initial` view for the newly selected seat unless the WebSocket pushes first.
   - Additionally, `[data-testid="player-row-${player.id}"]` in `GamePage.tsx` (lines 1515–1533) are plain `<li>` elements without tab/button roles (`role="tab"` / keyboard activation), and `GamePage.tsx` line 1880 passes `onCoverDrop` even when `hasLegalAction(displayedView.legalActions, 'cover')` is false (unlike `onTableCardClick` on line 1877), advertising `data-drop-zone="cover:<index>"` on attacker POVs.

## Required Changes
1. **`frontend/src/styles/table.css` & `frontend/src/components/GameTable.tsx`:**
   - Change `.hand-cards` from `grid-template-columns: auto; justify-content: center;` to `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr); justify-content: start;` (or ensure `.hand-highlight-contour` does not alter the grid track's intrinsic width, e.g., pinning column 1 to `100%` / `minmax(0, 1fr)` with `overflow: visible` on `.hand-cards` and `.hand-highlight-contour`).
   - Verify that when `hoveredCardIndex === 2` on a 6-card hand (`qa-normal-6-actions`), `.hand-row` does not shift horizontally, `card[2]` remains stationary (`0px` displacement), `card[1]` shifts left, and `card[3]` shifts right (`+14.67px`).
   - Refine `.hand-card-wrapper` transition in `frontend/src/styles/table.css` so hover-parting animates smoothly via GPU-composited `transform` (`transform 220ms cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`) without any layout-induced horizontal jumps.
2. **`frontend/src/pages/GamePage.tsx`:**
   - Fix line 505 so `getGameView` updates `view` whenever the fetched view's `myId` matches the requested `playerId` (`setView((current) => (current?.myId === playerId ? current : initial))`).
   - Give clickable player rows in `[data-testid="players-list"]` accessible tab/button semantics (`role={canSwitchPov ? 'tab' : undefined}`, `aria-selected={player.id === playerId ? 'true' : 'false'}`, `tabIndex={canSwitchPov ? 0 : undefined}`, and `onKeyDown` Enter/Space handling) while preserving `data-testid={`player-row-${player.id}`}` and all existing attributes/classes.
   - Gate `onCoverDrop` on `!reviewMode && hasLegalAction(displayedView.legalActions, 'cover') ? playCoverDrop : undefined` to match `onTableCardClick`.
