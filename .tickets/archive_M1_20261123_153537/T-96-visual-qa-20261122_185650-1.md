# T-96 Visual QA Fix: Felt Arena Pseudo-Element Inset, Trump Card Double-Border Size Shrink, and Favicon 404

## Summary of Objective Bugs Observed in Chromium Telemetry & Screenshots

1. **14-card and 30-card hands protrude past the visible felt surface of `[data-zone="table-arena"]` onto the grey page background (`step_7`, `step_11`, `step_12`, `step_13`, `step_14`):**
   - **Observed telemetry & pixel evidence:**
     - `div[data-zone="table-arena"]` has bounding box `{ x: 304, y: 127, w: 844, h: 796 }`, flush with `section[data-zone="hand-sort-box"]` below it (`{ x: 304, w: 844 }`).
     - In `frontend/src/styles/table.css` (lines 132 and 152–162), `[data-zone='table-arena']` has `padding: 24px 28px 28px 28px;`, and `[data-zone='table-arena']::before` (the felt surface layer) is placed via `grid-column: 1 / -1; grid-row: 1 / -1;`. Because CSS Grid items/pseudo-elements span the **content box** (inside padding) rather than the padding box, the painted felt surface is only **`788px` wide (`x: 332..1120`)** — leaving `28px` of transparent grey page background (`rgb(216, 221, 228)`) on both sides of `table-arena`.
     - Meanwhile, `frontend/src/components/GameTable.tsx` (lines 1607–1610) sizes `maximalBoxWidth` from `arenaWidth - HAND_LANE_MARGIN_PX * 2` (`844 - 32 = 812px`).
     - Consequently, in `step_11_item_5_14card_expand_box.png` and `step_12_item_5_30card_compress_box.png`, the hand cards (`x ≈ 318..1131`) and their highlight contour (`x ≈ 314..1136`) visibly overhang both left and right edges of the green felt onto the grey page background.
2. **Trump card double border shrinks `.playing-card.trump-card` SVGs to `98×138` instead of `100×140` (`step_11`, `step_12`, `visual_qa_runner.log` Item 5.3 & Item 5.4):**
   - **Observed telemetry & screenshot evidence:**
     - In `frontend/src/styles/table.css` (lines 1057–1075), `.card-slot.trump` sets `border: 1px solid var(--trump-accent);` on the `100×140` `box-sizing: border-box` wrapper, while `.playing-card.trump-card` inside it sets `width: 100%; height: 100%; border: 2px solid var(--trump-border, #f59e0b);`.
     - This squeezes every trump card's `<svg class="playing-card card-face trump-card">` down to **`98px × 138px`**, shifts its `left` edge by `+1px` (producing `maxStep=25.6` at the non-trump-to-trump boundary in `step_12`), paints a thicker 3px double border compared to non-trump cards (`step_11` `6♥, 7♥, 10♥` and `step_12` `7♥..A♥`), and causes `Item 5.3` and `Item 5.4` in `.reviews/visual_qa_runner.log` to fail the fixed `100×140` card-size invariant.
3. **Console 404 on initial page load (`visual_qa_telemetry.json`):**
   - `consoleErrors` reports `"Failed to load resource: the server responded with a status of 404 (Not Found)"` because `frontend/index.html` lacks a `<link rel="icon" ...>` tag and Chromium requests `/favicon.ico`.
