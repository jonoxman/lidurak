# T-95-audit-gap-3: Verify & Complete Tight Non-Rectangular Hand Contour, 20%–80% Card Overlap, Drag Visibility, and Multi-Select Throw-In

## Context

## Requirements
1. **Tight, Responsive, Non-Rectangular Active Hand Highlight Contour (New #1):**
   - Confirm the active player's hand highlight outline wraps tightly around the exact rendered bounding geometry of every card in `[data-zone="player-hand"]` at all times.
   - When playable cards shift upwards (or selected cards elevate), the contour must distort smoothly/accurately to hug the raised and unraised cards as a unified non-rectangular shape (never a stale or misaligned rectangular box).
   - Recalculate responsively on hand changes, window resize, sort order changes, playability shifts, and overlap adjustments.
2. **Dragged Cards Never Become Invisible (New #2):**
   - Confirm clicking and dragging a card never hides it (`opacity: 0`, `visibility: hidden`, or vanished drag image/source element). Every card remains visibly rendered throughout drag start, drag move, and drag end/drop (respecting normal z-index/overlap stacking).
3. **20%–80% Responsive Hand Card Overlap (New #3):**
   - Arrange cards in the active player's hand so adjacent cards overlap by approximately **20% minimum** to **80% maximum** of card width, dynamically tightening as hand size grows.
   - Always keep the left-side rank and suit index legible on every card.
   - Verify with `large-hand-stress` (30 cards), `huge-hand-36`, and `huge-hand-52` that even a full-deck pickup stays completely inside the viewport and `[data-zone="player-hand"]` without going off-screen or overlapping the talon deck, action bar, or sidebars, and that the tight contour from New #1 accurately hugs the overlapped layout.
4. **Drag-to-Outline & Multi-Select Double-Click Throw-In (New #4):**
   - Verify single legal throw-in cards can be deployed by dragging onto `[data-testid="throw-in-outline"]` OR double-clicking.
   - Verify players can single-click to toggle-select multiple legal throw-in cards (strictly restricted to `view.throwInCards` and capped at `view.throwInCapacity`) and double-click to atomically deploy all selected cards via `throwInCards`.
