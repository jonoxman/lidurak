Per `AGENTS.md` Rule #2, start by inspecting `git status`, `git diff`, and the live frontend layout—**preserve any already-working code** and remediate any overlap, clipping, or misplacement found.

## Requirements to Verify & Remediate
1. **Item #4 — Active Hand Timer Never Clipped or Overlapped:**
