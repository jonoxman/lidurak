# Audit Gap Verification & Remediation: Card Typography (`10` & Suit), Drag Offset, Option B Hand Sizing, Hover Parting & Two-Tier Raise (Items #2, #3, #5, #6, #8)

## Context
During the final pre-release audit on `main`, frontend source and stylesheet files were truncated from the audit diff/file payload and could not be verified live in the UI. Per `AGENTS.md` Rule #2, start by inspecting `git status`, `git diff`, and the current frontend implementation—**preserve any already-working code** and fix every missing, divergent, or unwired detail below.

## Requirements to Verify & Remediate
1. **Item #2 — Consistent `'10'` Rank Font Size, Overlap Adjustment & Larger Side Suit Indicator:**
   - Ensure the `'10'` rank glyph on playing cards renders at the exact same font size as all other numeric ranks (`2`–`9`), never shrunken.
   - Adjust the maximum allowable card overlap in the active hand (and any other overlapped card strip) so the entire `'10'` rank remains unoccluded while in the allowable-overlap regime.
   - Increase the side/corner suit indicator size to make full use of the widened exposed left card strip.
2. **Item #3 — Zero Horizontal Drag Offset:**
   - Verify clicking and dragging any card from `[data-zone="player-hand"]` keeps the dragged card centered/anchored accurately to the pointer with **zero horizontal offset**, including for cards near the far right of normal, 14-card, and 30-card hands.
   - Ensure the dragged card remains visibly rendered throughout the entire drag lifecycle (do not regress visibility).
3. **Item #5 (Human Clarification: Option B) — Fixed Card Size, Default 6-Card Box, Maximal Box & Progressive Compression:**
   - Never shrink card dimensions in large hands (cards keep their full standard dimensions regardless of hand size).
   - Constrain normal hands (up to 6 cards) to the default box width equal to a 6-card hand with standard overlap.
   - Define the maximal hand box spanning almost the full width of the play area with small symmetric margins on both sides.
   - Enforce **Option B**: as hand size increases past the default 6-card box, keep full card size and expand the hand width toward the maximal box while maintaining full-visibility allowable overlap (full rank including `'10'` and suit visible); only after the hand at maximal allowable overlap would exceed the maximal box width, compress overlap further beyond the allowable threshold (allowing partial occlusion of rank/suit) so the hand remains strictly inside the maximal box without overflowing the viewport or colliding with adjacent zones.
4. **Item #6 — Smooth Mouse-Over Hand Parting Animation:**
   - Hovering over `[data-zone="player-hand"]` smoothly animates "parting" the cards: reducing overlap for cards close to the mouse pointer while increasing overlap for cards farther away, keeping total hand bounds clean and aesthetically smooth.
   - **1-card guard:** When the hand has only 1 card, the parting animation MUST NOT run.
   - **Anchored hovered card:** The card directly under the cursor MUST NOT change its horizontal/vertical position due to the parting animation.
5. **Item #8 — Two-Tier Vertical Card Raise & Highlight Contour:**
   - Eligible/playable cards are vertically raised **halfway** to the full lift height.
   - User-selected cards are vertically raised **fully** to the full lift height.
   - The non-rectangular responsive hand highlight contour wraps tightly around unraised, half-raised (eligible), and full-raised (selected) cards across normal and large hands.
