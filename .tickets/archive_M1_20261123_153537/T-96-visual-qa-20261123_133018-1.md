# Fix Hover Hit-Testing in Parted Large Hands (`GameTable.tsx`)

## Visual QA Evidence & Root Cause
In the Chromium visual QA run (`30-Card Compress & Part` at `1280x800`), hovering a card in a compressed 30-card hand (`step ≈ 19.03px`) shifts its immediate right neighbours rightward (`deltas[i + 1] ≈ +22.8px`, expanding card `i`'s visible width to `~41.85px` and pushing card `i + 1`'s rendered left edge past `baseCardPositions[i + 2]`).

However, `handPointerIndex` in `frontend/src/components/GameTable.tsx` (lines 1695–1712) currently resolves the hovered index against **resting** `baseCardPositions`:
```ts
return resolveHoveredCardIndex(
  pointer.clientX,
  baseCardPositions,
  cardWidth,
  rect.left,
);
```

Because `baseCardPositions` ignores the live single-sided rightward parting offsets:
1. When card `15` is hovered (`.reviews/screenshots/step_2_item_1_hover_card_15_single_right_partin.png`), card `16` is pushed rightward past `baseCardPositions[17]`. Moving the pointer onto card `16`'s visible strip resolves to card `17` instead of card `16`, so card `17` opens (`next=41.85`) while card `16` stays narrow (`19.05px`) — observed in `.reviews/screenshots/step_2_item_1_sweep_hover_card_16.png`.
2. Moving the pointer onto the middle of card `17`'s widened `41.85px` visible strip (`rc.left + 20.9px > baseCardPositions[18]`) resolves to card `18` instead of card `17`, closing card `17` (`19.03px`) and opening card `18` (`41.87px`) — observed in `.reviews/screenshots/step_2_item_1_sweep_hover_card_17.png`.
3. Consequently, the card revealed with the widest margin is not the card under the mouse pointer during a sweep across a large compressed hand.

## Required Fix
1. **Resolve hover hit-testing against live rendered card positions (`frontend/src/components/GameTable.tsx`):**
   - Track the latest non-drag card `x` positions (`partingActive ? partedCardLayout.positions : baseCardPositions`) in a ref (e.g., `renderedHoverPositionsRef.current`) updated each render, so both synchronous leading-edge `handleHandPointerMove` calls and the trailing `requestAnimationFrame` callback read up-to-date rendered positions without stale closures.
   - Update `handPointerIndex` to pass `renderedHoverPositionsRef.current` (instead of `baseCardPositions`) into `resolveHoveredCardIndex(pointer.clientX, renderedHoverPositionsRef.current, cardWidth, rect.left)` when a real laid-out hand bounding rect is present.
   - Verify no oscillation can occur: with single-sided rightward parting (`deltas[0..h] === 0`, `deltas[h+1..h+radius] > 0`), once card `j` is hovered, `rendered_new[j] = base[j] <= x < rendered_new[j + 1]` holds for all `j` (whether sweeping left-to-right or right-to-left).
2. **Update comment/documentation in `frontend/src/utils/handLayout.ts` (around `resolveHoveredCardIndex`, lines 1064–1080):**
   - Clarify that with single-sided right parting, `resolveHoveredCardIndex` safely operates on the live rendered/parted positions without oscillation, ensuring the visually exposed strip under the pointer always matches the selected card.

## Acceptance Criteria & Tests
- Add/update tests in `frontend/src/__tests__/GameTable.test.tsx` and `frontend/src/__tests__/handLayout.test.ts`:
  - In a 30-card compressed hand (`maximalBoxWidth = 652`, `cardWidth = 100`), hover card `15`, then move the pointer into the visible strip of card `16`'s rendered bounding box (`partedCardLayout.positions[16] + 5px`) and assert `data-hovered-card-index="16"` (card `16` has the strictly widest exposed width, not card `17`).
  - Sweep rightward into the middle of card `17`'s widened rendered strip and assert `data-hovered-card-index="17"` with zero oscillation on repeated pointer moves at the same or neighbouring coordinates.
