# T-95-audit-gap-2: Verify & Complete Throw-In Outline Gating, Card-to-Outline Size Parity, and QA Scenario Preset Wiring

## Context
The backend (`PlayerGameView.scala`, `GameManager.scala`) projects `canThrowIn`, `throwInCards`, and `throwInCapacity` and registers the `throw-in-multi-qa` and `large-hand-stress` presets, but the corresponding frontend rendering and CSS sizing were not verifiable in the audit bundle. Inspect existing files on `main` first (preserve working code) and remediate any unwired or mismatched frontend behavior for **QA Playbook #1**, **QA Playbook #2**, and **QA Scenario Preset wiring**.

## Requirements
1. **Strict Conditional Visibility of Throw-In Outline (QA Playbook #1):**
   - Render `[data-testid="throw-in-outline"]` (both inline and in the surrender above/below anchor) **only** when the active viewer can legally play into it (`view.canThrowIn && view.throwInCards.length > 0`, and never in review mode or for a bot seat).
   - Verify it is hidden when:
     - the viewer holds no valid rank to throw in,
     - the viewer is the defending player,
     - the viewer has already passed,
     - or the bout is at capacity.
2. **Playing Card Size Matches the Larger Outlines (QA Playbook #2):**
   - Verify rendered playing cards (both on the battlefield table and in the active player's hand) match the larger dimensions of `[data-testid="throw-in-outline"]` and `[data-testid="redirect-outline"]` (exact width, height, and border-radius parity on desktop and mobile breakpoints), rather than rendering substantially smaller than the outlines.
   - Ensure CSS Grid/Flexbox rules prevent flex shrinking or stretching mismatches between cards and outlines.
3. **1-Click QA Scenario Preset Wiring:**
   - Verify both new backend presets (`throw-in-multi-qa` and `large-hand-stress`) — along with existing throw-in/hand presets — are wired and clickable in the QA Scenarios panel on both `LobbyPage.tsx` and the left sidebar of `GamePage.tsx`.
