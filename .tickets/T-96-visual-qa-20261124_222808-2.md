# Align M2 Lobby Selection Chrome with Milestone 1 Style & Fix Active-Match QA Preset Turn

## Context & Observed Defects
1. **M2 lobby selection chrome diverges from Milestone 1 lobby styling (`<implemented_feedback>` Item #2):**
   - In `frontend/src/app.css`, Milestone 1 explicitly defines the Royal Blue / Cobalt selection family (`--ui-select-accent`, `--ui-select-border`, `--ui-select-glow`, `--ui-select-bg`, `--ui-select-text`) for lobby form controls, active selections, and UI chrome (`Never green...`), and `HeaderBar` `Theme -> Palettes` presets dynamically re-color `--ui-select-*` (the `uiSelect` slot).
   - However, in `frontend/src/styles/lobby.css`, the newly created M2 lobby elements (`Quick Pairing`, `Lobby Pool`, `"Create a Game"`) define `--m2-green: var(--app-accent);` and apply green (`#629924` / `#4f7d1c`) to `.m2-lobby-tab.active`, `.quick-pair-tile:hover`, `.quick-pair-searching`, `.segmented-option[aria-pressed='true']`, `.create-invite-toggle input`, and `.m2-button` (`"Create room"`).
   - As visible in `step_5`, `step_6`, `step_7`, `step_10`, `step_11`, and `step_12`, the M2 `"Create a Game"` panel uses green controls while the M1 `Local game` panel directly below it on the same page uses cobalt blue (`--ui-select-accent`).

2. **`active-match` QA preset stalls turn 1 on a synthetic opponent (`step_13`–`step_15`):**
   - In `backend/src/main/scala/durak/http/QaPresetRoutes.scala`, `activeMatch` deals with `PresetSeed = 20261123L` and seats the caller in seat 0 against `qa-opp-1` (a synthetic session with no bot AI or WebSocket client). Under that deal, `qa-opp-1` holds the lowest trump and is `attackerIdx = 1`, so `step_13`, `step_14`, and `step_15` all show an empty bout area where the caller has no legal actions and the synthetic opponent never plays.

## Required Changes
1. **`frontend/src/styles/lobby.css` & `frontend/src/pages/LobbyPage.test.tsx`:**
   - Replace the green selection/interactive chrome in `.m2-lobby` with Milestone 1's `--ui-select-*` custom properties (`--ui-select-accent`, `--ui-select-border`, `--ui-select-glow`, `--ui-select-bg`, `--ui-select-text`) for:
     - Active/hovered lobby tabs (`.m2-lobby-tab.active`)
     - Quick Pairing tile hover/focus (`.quick-pair-tile:hover`, `.quick-pair-tile:focus-visible`)
     - `"Create a Game"` segmented controls (`.segmented-option[aria-pressed='true']`, `.segmented-option:hover`), checkbox (`accent-color: var(--ui-select-accent)`), and primary submit button (`.m2-button.primary`, matching `.lobby-form .action-button.green` in `app.css`)
     - Secondary button hover/focus (`.m2-button.secondary:hover`) and guest badge/input focus states
   - Keep `--app-bg`, `--app-panel`, `--bg-elevated`, `--app-border`, `--app-text`, `--app-text-strong`, `--app-roundness`, and `--app-shadow` theme-reactive across both `dark` and `light` themes (and `Theme -> Palettes`).
   - Update `frontend/src/pages/LobbyPage.test.tsx` assertions that previously checked for `--m2-green: var(--app-accent)` so they verify the M1 `--ui-select-*` tokens and theme reactivity instead.

2. **`backend/src/main/scala/durak/http/QaPresetRoutes.scala` (and `backend/src/test/scala/durak/http/MultiplayerRoutesSpec.scala`):**
   - Update `activeMatch` so the preset deals from a deterministic seed (or swaps initial attacker / seat deal) where seat `0` (the caller) is the initial attacker (`attackerIdx == 0`, `legalActions` contains `"attack"`), so clicking `"Launch active projected match"` (or `"Launch small-hand match (4 cards)"`) immediately renders playable cards, target outlines, and the `Attack` button for the reviewer.

  - Launching `"Launch active projected match"` immediately gives the caller the opening attack turn and lets them play an attack card into the bout.
