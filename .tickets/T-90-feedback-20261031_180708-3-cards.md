### 1. Suit Color Contrast (`frontend/src/components/Card.tsx`)

```ts
export function suitColor(suit: string): string {
  return isRedSuit(suit) ? '#c33' : '#111111'; // Pure sharply contrasting black
}
```

### 2. Card Sprites & Pip Layouts (`frontend/src/components/Card.tsx`)
- **Ranks 2 to 10:** Render SVG pips arranged according to traditional card geometries:
  - 2: Top-center, bottom-center.
  - 3: Top-center, center, bottom-center.
  - 4: 4 corners.
  - 5: 4 corners + 1 center pip.
  - 6: 2 columns of 3.
  - 7: 2 columns of 3 + 1 upper-center pip.
  - 8: 2 columns of 3 + 2 center pips (upper & lower).
  - 9: 2 columns of 4 + 1 center pip.
  - 10: 2 columns of 4 + 2 center pips.
- **Ace:** Render a single large, ornate suit pip centered at `x="35" y="50"` with width/height ~35px (~50% of the 70px card width).
- **Jack, Queen, King:** Render large stylized letter (`J`, `Q`, `K`) centered in the face area, with smaller decorative suit pips placed symmetrically around the letter.
- Corner indices: Small rank and suit symbol in top-left and inverted in bottom-right corners.

  - The vertical talon deck overlaps the horizontal trump card perpendicularly at its center, leaving the trump card's rank and suit visible on both sides of the talon deck.
- Maintain CSS Grid containment without absolute positioning outside the deck zone.

### 5. Scalable Card Sizing & Target Outlines
- Scale default card dimensions (e.g. from 70x100 to 76x108 or 80x114 via CSS variables `--card-width: 76px; --card-height: 108px;`).

### 6. Card Back Texture Catalog (`frontend/src/components/CardBack.tsx`)
Support at least 6 distinct card back patterns selectable by name (`pattern?: string`):
1. `classic-hatch`: Diagonal criss-cross hatch pattern.
2. `royal-diamonds`: Elegant interlocking diamond lattice.
3. `celtic-knot`: Decorative geometric interlaced curves.
4. `vintage-stars`: Repeating 8-point geometric star field.
5. `hex-grid`: Modern honeycomb hexagonal weave.
6. `velvet-damask`: Subtle ornate heraldic flourish pattern.
