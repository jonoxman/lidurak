### README.md: add or update user-facing sections
- **Throw-in controls:**
  - The Throw In button is gone.
  - Drag a legal card onto the dashed additional-card outline, or double-click it.
  - To throw in several cards, click to select legal cards (they lift and glow), then double-click to deploy them all at once.
  - The outline appears only for players who can legally throw in. It never appears for the defender, for players with no valid cards, or after passing.
- **Action buttons:** Surrender and Pass are compact icon-only themed buttons in the bottom-right of the table. Describe both icons (surrender: card fan, down arrow, two raised hands; pass: card fan, open hand, sideways arrow). Accessible names are "Surrender" / "Pass".
- **Cards and hand:**
  - Cards are 100×140 on desktop (48×68 below 900px) and match all outlines.
  - Hand cards overlap by about 20–80% and always keep the corner index visible.
  - Large hands wrap into rows.
  - The active-hand outline hugs the hand's real shape, including raised playable cards.
  - Cards stay visible while dragging.
- **⚡ QA Scenarios:** add `throw-in-visibility-3p`, `multi-throw-in` and `full-hand-4p` with a one-line "what to check" for each, alongside the existing presets.
