## Implementation Spec

### 1. `README.md`
Update the "How to Play" and controls sections:
- **Throwing in:** there is no Throw In button.
  - Drag a legal card onto the dashed emerald outline, or double-click it.
  - Click several legal cards to select them, then double-click to throw them all in at once.
  - Selection is capped by the remaining bout capacity.
- **Outline visibility:** the throw-in outline appears only for a player who can legally throw in. It is hidden for the defender, for a player who has passed, and for a player with no matching ranks.
- **Icon buttons:**
  - Surrender: hands raised under cards, with a downward arrow.
  - Pass: a palm-down wave-off under cards, with a sideways arrow.
  - Both sit in the bottom-right corner of the table.
- **Hand:**
  - Cards overlap by 20–80%, and the corner index is always visible.
  - Very large hands wrap into rows.
  - A silhouette outline hugs the hand.
  - Cards stay visible while being dragged.
- **⚡ QA Scenarios list:** add `throw-in-visibility-4p`, `throw-in-capacity-cap`, `huge-hand-36` and `huge-hand-52`, each with one line on what to test.
