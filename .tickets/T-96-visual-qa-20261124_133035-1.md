# T-96-visual-qa-20261124_133035-1: Fix Quick Pairing Queue Persistence, "Create a Game" Lobby Layout, Tile Height, and M1 Theme Adoption

## Visual & Telemetry Findings
Inspection of `.reviews/visual_qa_telemetry.json` and `.reviews/screenshots/` against `<implemented_feedback>` reveals three unimplemented/failing lobby and matchmaking requirements:

1. **QA #6 — Quick Pairing Immediately Enters a Room Instead of Waiting in Queue (`step_1_qa_6_searching_persistent.png`):**
   - Telemetry step `qa_6_searching_persistent` is at URL `http://localhost:5173/g/ByFaz4z7`, and `step_1_qa_6_searching_persistent.png` renders `<WaitingRoom>` (`Room ByFaz4z7`, `1 / 2`, `Shareable invite link`, `Seat 1 Guest-1IA4`, `Seat 2 Open seat`, `Leave Room`).
   - Root cause: `QuickPairingService.pairLocked` (`backend/src/main/scala/durak/lobby/QuickPairingService.scala`) immediately calls `roomManager.createRoom(session, config)` and returns `(room.gameId, seatIndex)` via `POST /api/quick-pair` (`backend/src/main/scala/durak/http/MultiplayerRoutes.scala`). In `frontend/src/components/lobby/QuickPairingTab.tsx`, `start()` awaits `joinQuickPair(preset)` and immediately calls `onMatched(matched.gameId)`, which invokes `navigateToGame(gameId)` and exits the lobby.
   - Meanwhile, `/ws/lobby` in `MultiplayerRoutes.scala` drains incoming frames without authenticating the guest session or pushing `WsServerMessage.MatchFound(gameId)` to queued players.

2. **New #1 — Quick Pairing Tile Height, Section Rename ("Create a Game"), and Form Ordering (`step_1_lobby_initial.png`, `step_4_item_1_lobby_initial.png`, `step_5_item_1_create_a_game_form.png`):**
   - `.quick-pair-tile` in `frontend/src/styles/lobby.css` (`padding: 0.85rem 0.75rem`) renders at ~65px height instead of roughly double its height while preserving the 3x2 grid layout.
   - `LobbyPage.tsx` (`LOBBY_TABS`) and `CreateInviteTab.tsx` still label the third tab and panel `"Create Invite Link"` instead of `"Create a Game"`.
   - `CreateInviteTab.tsx` still places the Visibility selector (`Private invite link` / `Public lobby pool`) at the bottom of the form after Players, Redirect / reflect, and Time control per turn, instead of first.

3. **New #2 — Milestone 1 Styling & Profile Menu Theme Responsiveness (`step_1_lobby_initial.png`, `step_11_item_2_light_theme_searching.png`, `step_12_item_2_light_theme_create_form.png`):**
   - `frontend/src/styles/lobby.css` hardcodes dark-only `--m2-*` and `--mp-*` color variables (`#161512`, `#262421`, `#bababa`, `#f1f1f1`) on `.m2-lobby` and `.multiplayer-page`, ignoring `html[data-theme='light']`, `@media (prefers-color-scheme: light)`, `--app-roundness`, and M1's `--app-*` / `--ui-select-*` tokens from `frontend/src/app.css`.
   - In `step_1_lobby_initial.png`, the M2 lobby renders as a stark dark `#161512` card above the Light-themed M1 `Local game` panel. In `step_11_item_2_light_theme_searching.png` and `step_12_item_2_light_theme_create_form.png`, toggling Light theme in the Profile menu leaves `.multiplayer-page` dark.

## Required Fixes
1. **Quick Pairing Queue Behavior (Backend + Frontend):**
   - `backend/src/main/scala/durak/lobby/QuickPairingService.scala` & `backend/src/main/scala/durak/http/MultiplayerRoutes.scala`:
     - Keep players who call `POST /api/quick-pair` in an in-memory pairing queue (keyed by preset `(playerCount, turnTimeoutSeconds, allowRedirect)` and `session.playerId`) rather than creating/seating them in an active `GameRoom` before `playerCount` players have matched.
     - Only once `playerCount` queued players exist for a preset, atomically create the `GameRoom` and seat all matched players, then emit `WsServerMessage.MatchFound(gameId)` to each matched player over `/ws/lobby` (identify the `/ws/lobby` viewer via `SessionCookie.extractToken` / `?token=` just like `/ws/games/:gameId`) and/or return status in a queue status response if needed.
     - Ensure `DELETE /api/quick-pair` removes the session from the pairing queue cleanly, and disconnect/cleanup removes stale queued players when appropriate without creating orphan rooms.
   - `frontend/src/components/lobby/QuickPairingTab.tsx` & `frontend/src/pages/LobbyPage.tsx` & `frontend/src/api/lobbyClient.ts`:
     - When `joinQuickPair(preset)` returns a `queued` / pending state (no `gameId` yet), stay on the Lobby screen (`/`), keep `[data-testid="quick-pair-searching"]` visible with the `Cancel` button, and subscribe to `/ws/lobby` `onMatchFound` (which already exists on `LobbySocketHandlers`) to call `onMatched(gameId)` only when a full match is found.
     - Clicking `Cancel` must call `cancelQuickPair()` (`DELETE /api/quick-pair`) and return the lobby tab to idle without navigating.

2. **Lobby Tile Sizing & "Create a Game" Tab Reordering:**
   - `frontend/src/styles/lobby.css`: Double the height of `.quick-pair-tile` (e.g., `min-height: 132px; align-content: center;`) while keeping the existing 3-column desktop / 2-column mobile grid layout and internal title/meta structure unchanged.
   - `frontend/src/pages/LobbyPage.tsx`: Rename `{ id: 'invite', label: 'Create Invite Link' }` to `{ id: 'invite', label: 'Create a Game' }`.
   - `frontend/src/components/lobby/CreateInviteTab.tsx` (and `LobbyPoolTab.tsx` button/docs if applicable):
     - Update the `<section aria-label>` and `<h2 class="m2-panel-title">` to `"Create a Game"`.
     - Move the Visibility choice (`Private invite link` vs `Public lobby pool` — whether the game is a private link or not) to be the **first** field in `.create-invite-form`, followed by `Players`, `Redirect / reflect`, and `Time control per turn` in their current order.

3. **Adopt Milestone 1 Visual Style & Theme Support:**
   - `frontend/src/styles/lobby.css`:
     - Replace hardcoded dark `--m2-*` and `--mp-*` values with M1 CSS custom properties from `frontend/src/app.css` (`--app-bg`, `--app-panel`, `--app-input-bg`, `--app-border`, `--app-text`, `--app-text-strong`, `--app-roundness`, `--ui-select-accent`, `--ui-select-border`, `--ui-select-glow`, `--ui-select-bg`, `--ui-select-text`) so `.m2-lobby`, `.m2-panel`, `.m2-lobby-tab`, `.quick-pair-tile`, `.quick-pair-searching`, `.lobby-pool-row`, `.create-invite-form`, `.segmented-option`, `.qa-lobby-presets`, `.m2-button`, and `.mp-waiting` visually match the Milestone 1 UI and respond immediately to Light, Dark, Device theme, and Roundness settings in `HeaderBar`'s Profile menu.
