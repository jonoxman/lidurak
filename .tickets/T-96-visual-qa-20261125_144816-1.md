# Fix Crushed Center Opponent Seat Width and Vertical Box Clipping on `GameTable`

## Problem Summary
In 2-player live matches rendered at `1280×800` (observed in `.reviews/screenshots/step_7_item_6_quick_pair_matched_game_page.png`, `step_17_item_3_m1_active_match_table.png`, `step_18_item_3_after_opening_attack.png`, `step_21_item_4_small_hand_resting.png`, `step_22_item_4_small_hand_hover_index_1.png`, `step_23_item_4_small_hand_hover_index_3_sweep.png`, and `step_24_item_4_small_hand_mouse_off.png`), the single opponent seat in Row 1 (`.seat-top-center`) is squeezed horizontally to ~132px and clipped vertically inside `.seat-box`:
1. **Center seat column crushed by empty outer tracks:** In `frontend/src/styles/table.css` (`[data-zone='table-arena']`), `grid-template-columns: minmax(240px, 1fr) minmax(0, 2fr) minmax(240px, 1fr)` unconditionally reserves `240px` for Column 1 and Column 3 (`480px` total) even when those columns have no seats (2P games) or when the arena is narrower than `720px` + gaps (`~612px` content width on a `1280×800` viewport with `.game-shell` sidebars). This crushes Column 2 (`.seat-top-center`) to `~132px` instead of `240px`, violating seat-size uniformity and leaving only `~82px` of text width beside the avatar.
2. **Mid-word wrapping & vertical clipping inside `.seat-box`:** Because `.opponent-name` has `overflow-wrap: anywhere` inside the crushed `~132px` `.seat-top-center` box, `"QA Opponent 1"` wraps mid-word across 3 lines (`QA` / `Oppone` / `nt 1`) and `"QA Quick Pair Opponent 1"` wraps across 5 lines. At `800px` viewport height, `--seat-row-height: clamp(116px, 14.5vh, 150px)` resolves to `116px` (`~91px` for `.seat-box` after `.seat-clock` and gap). Because `.seat-box` has `justify-content: center; overflow: hidden;` and `.opponent-hand` has `min-height: 44px`, the wrapped name overflows both vertically upward and downward: the top of `"QA"` / `"Quick"` is clipped off at the top edge of `.seat-box`, and the opponent mini-cards are clipped off at the bottom edge of `.seat-box`.

## Required Changes

### 1. Prevent empty or narrow outer columns from crushing `.seat-top-center` (`frontend/src/components/GameTable.tsx`, `frontend/src/styles/table.css`)
- Expose `data-opponent-count={opponents.length}` (or seat layout mode) on `[data-zone='table-arena']` in `frontend/src/components/GameTable.tsx` (around line 2950), and update `frontend/src/styles/table.css`:
  - When there is only 1 opponent (`[data-zone='table-arena'][data-opponent-count='1']`, where only `.seat-top-center` is rendered in Row 1 and Columns 1 & 3 are empty), use `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr) minmax(240px, 2fr) minmax(0, 1fr);` so `.seat-top-center` keeps its full `240px` `--seat-width`.
  - When 3 opponents are present (`data-opponent-count='3'`, all three top seats rendered) on desktop viewports where `[data-zone='table-arena']` content width is below `~760px` (e.g., `@media (min-width: 900px) and (max-width: 1420px)`), use `grid-template-columns: repeat(3, minmax(0, 1fr));` so all three seats share equal width rather than keeping outer seats at `240px` while crushing `.seat-top-center` to `~132px`.
  - Keep the base `[data-zone='table-arena']` rule / wide-desktop behavior intact for 2-opponent layouts (`.seat-top-left` + `.seat-top-right`, as in `step_19_item_3_spectator_match_table.png`).

### 2. Prevent mid-word splits and vertical clipping inside `.seat-box` (`frontend/src/styles/table.css`)
- On `.opponent-name` (line 325): change `overflow-wrap: anywhere;` to `overflow-wrap: break-word;` (keeping `white-space: normal; min-width: 0; overflow: visible; text-overflow: clip;`) and set a compact `font-size: 13px; line-height: 1.2;` so multi-word names like `"QA Opponent 1"` and `"QA Quick Pair Opponent 1"` wrap cleanly at word boundaries without breaking `"Opponent"` mid-word.
- Ensure `.seat-box` never clips its name or mini-cards even when a long name wraps to 2 lines at `800px` viewport height (`--seat-row-height: 116px`), while preserving the `1280×800` total arena height assertion (`<= 800px`) in `frontend/src/__tests__/GameTable.test.tsx`:
  - Tighten `.seat` gap from `4px` to `2px`, `.seat-box` padding from `8px 10px` to `4px 10px` and gap from `6px` to `4px`, and `.opponent-hand` `min-height` from `44px` to `36px` (mini-cards are `32px` tall) with `justify-content: center` (or `flex-start` when content fills height) so a 2-line `.opponent-name` + `.opponent-hand` fits completely inside `~91px` `.seat-box` with zero top or bottom clipping.

  - Verify `.opponent-name` wraps at word boundaries (`overflow-wrap: break-word`) and that `.seat-box` vertical layout fits a 2-line name + `.opponent-hand` within the `116px` `--seat-row-height` budget at `1280×800` without breaking the existing `keeps the arena, the hand and the controls inside a 1280x800 viewport` test.
