# Ticket 4: BasicBot Implementation & Lobby Dropdown Integration

## Summary
Implement a more sophisticated bot, `BasicBot`, in the backend engine and server, and add `BasicBot` to the Lobby player type dropdown below `RandomBot`:
- `BasicBot` must always play legal moves.
- When defending, `BasicBot` must always try to defend (`CoverCard`) or redirect (`RedirectAttack`) rather than surrendering (`DeclareSurrender`), whenever a legal defense or redirect exists.
- On its own turn to attack (`InitialAttack`), `BasicBot` must always play the lowest-ranked non-trump card in its hand if possible; if it only holds trump cards, it plays a random trump card instead.
- If another player surrenders (`BoutPhase.Surrendered`), `BasicBot` must throw in any cards that are legal to play as long as they are **not** trumps, and pass once no legal non-trump throw-ins remain.

## Requirements

### 1. `BasicBot` Decision Logic (`backend/src/main/scala/durak/engine/BasicBot.scala`)
- Expose `BasicBot.legalActionsFor(state: GameState, playerId: String): Vector[GameAction]` (delegating to `RandomBot.legalActionsFor(state, playerId)` so all moves are guaranteed legal under `GameEngine.applyAction`) and `BasicBot.chooseAction(state: GameState, playerId: String, rng: Random = new Random()): Option[GameAction]`.
- **Opening Attack (`InitialAttack`):**
  - Collect all legal `GameAction.InitialAttack(card)` moves for `playerId`.
  - Separate them into non-trump cards (`card.suit != state.trumpSuit`) and trump cards (`card.suit == state.trumpSuit`).
  - If one or more non-trump attack moves exist, select the card with the **lowest rank** (`card.rank.value` / `card.rank.ordinal`). (If multiple non-trump cards share the exact same lowest rank, pick among those lowest-rank cards via `rng`.)
  - If the bot holds only trump cards (no non-trump `InitialAttack` exists), pick a random trump `InitialAttack(card)` using `rng`.
- **Defending (`CoverCard` / `RedirectAttack` vs `DeclareSurrender`):**
  - When `playerId` is the defender facing uncovered cards, check all legal `CoverCard` and `RedirectAttack` moves.
  - If at least one legal `CoverCard` or `RedirectAttack` exists, `BasicBot` **must never** choose `DeclareSurrender`. Prefer non-trump covers/redirects or lowest-cost legal cover/redirect (e.g. lowest rank non-trump first, then lowest rank trump).
  - Only choose `DeclareSurrender` when no legal `CoverCard` or `RedirectAttack` move is available.
- **Throwing In When Another Player Surrenders (`BoutPhase.Surrendered`) & General Throw-Ins:**
  - When `state.boutPhase == BoutPhase.Surrendered` (another player has surrendered) or during throw-in opportunities:
  - Filter legal `ThrowInCards(cards)` moves to those containing **only non-trump cards** (`cards.nonEmpty && cards.forall(_.suit != state.trumpSuit)`).
  - If any legal non-trump `ThrowInCards` move exists, `BasicBot` must play a non-trump `ThrowInCards` move (choosing the lowest-ranked non-trump card first) rather than `PassTurn`.
  - `BasicBot` must **never** throw in trump cards when another player surrenders; if only trump `ThrowInCards` moves (or no `ThrowInCards` moves) remain, `BasicBot` chooses `PassTurn`.

### 2. Backend Config, Actor & Routes Integration (`GameConfig.scala`, `GameActor.scala`, `GameRoutes.scala`)
- In `GameConfig.scala`:
  - Update `normalizePlayerTypes` to accept `"basicBot"` (as well as `"randomBot"` and `"human"`).
  - Ensure `isBotSeat(idx)` returns `true` for both `"randomBot"` and `"basicBot"`.
  - Provide a helper (e.g. `seatType(idx): String`) so `GameActor` can distinguish `"basicBot"` from `"randomBot"`.
- In `GameActor.scala`:
  - When a bot seat is prompted, dispatch to `BasicBot.chooseAction(state, playerId, rng)` if the seat type is `"basicBot"`, and `RandomBot.chooseAction(state, playerId, rng)` if `"randomBot"`.
- In `GameRoutes.scala`:
  - Accept and preserve `"basicBot"` in `playerTypes` when creating games via `POST /api/games` (`POST /api/game/create`).

### 3. Frontend Types & Lobby Dropdown (`types.ts`, `client.ts`, `LobbyPage.tsx`)
- In `frontend/src/types.ts` (and `client.ts` if typed there), expand `PlayerType` to `'human' | 'randomBot' | 'basicBot'`.
- In `frontend/src/pages/LobbyPage.tsx`, add `<option value="basicBot">BasicBot</option>` directly below `<option value="randomBot">RandomBot</option>` in each player's seat type dropdown.
