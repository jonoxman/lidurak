# T-96-visual-qa-20261125_173735-2: Display Actual 52-Card / 36-Card `deckSize` in Lobby Pool and Game Room UI

## Problem
Item 6 of `<implemented_feedback>` adds a 52-card deck setting for 2-player and 3-player games. While the backend already includes `"deckSize"` in `RoomConfigView` ([ProtocolCodecs.scala](file:///home/shade_of_ox/lidurak-agent-project/backend/src/main/scala/durak/protocol/ProtocolCodecs.scala#L29-L60)) and `LobbyRoomSummary`, the frontend drops and ignores `deckSize` when rendering room metadata:
1. [multiplayer.ts](file:///home/shade_of_ox/lidurak-agent-project/frontend/src/types/multiplayer.ts#L68-L73) omits `deckSize` from `RoomConfigView` and `parseRoomConfigView` ([lines 365-380](file:///home/shade_of_ox/lidurak-agent-project/frontend/src/types/multiplayer.ts#L365-L380)).
2. `deckLabel(playerCount: number)` in [multiplayer.ts](file:///home/shade_of_ox/lidurak-agent-project/frontend/src/types/multiplayer.ts#L562-L564) hardcodes `playerCount === 4 ? '52-card' : '36-card'`.
3. [GamePage.tsx](file:///home/shade_of_ox/lidurak-agent-project/frontend/src/pages/GamePage.tsx#L2332) (waiting-room badge) and [line 2819](file:///home/shade_of_ox/lidurak-agent-project/frontend/src/pages/GamePage.tsx#L2819) (in-game `ROOM` sidebar panel) call `deckLabel(room.config.playerCount)`, causing 2P and 3P 52-card rooms to display `"36-card deck"`.
4. [LobbyPoolTab.tsx](file:///home/shade_of_ox/lidurak-agent-project/frontend/src/components/lobby/LobbyPoolTab.tsx#L104) renders `{room.playerCount === 4 ? '52-card' : '36-card'} deck`, ignoring `room.deckSize`.

## Required Fix
1. Add optional/parsed `deckSize?: DeckSize` (or `number`, defaulting via `defaultDeckSize(playerCount)` when omitted by older payloads) to `RoomConfigView` and `parseRoomConfigView` in `frontend/src/types/multiplayer.ts`.
2. Update `deckLabel(playerCount: number, deckSize?: number)` in `frontend/src/types/multiplayer.ts` to prefer `deckSize` when provided (`(deckSize ?? defaultDeckSize(playerCount)) === 52 ? '52-card' : '36-card'`).
3. Pass `room.config.deckSize` to `deckLabel` in both the waiting room and in-game `ROOM` panel in `frontend/src/pages/GamePage.tsx`, and use `deckLabel(room.playerCount, room.deckSize)` in `frontend/src/components/lobby/LobbyPoolTab.tsx`.
