# Fix In-Hand Drag Reorder Slot Drift on Small Hands (Re-Centering Offset & Boundary Check)

## Problem Summary
During Chromium visual QA of in-hand drag parting (`qa-normal-6-actions`, 6 cards), dragging card `0` (`6♦`) to the visual midpoint between card `1` (`9♦`) and card `2` (`10♣`) (`pointerX = 146`) fails to shift the parting gap to insertion slot `1`, visually overlaps the dragged card on top of `10♣`, and commits `6♦` to `data-index="2"` instead of `data-index="1"`:
- Telemetry (`Item 3 Step 6`): `idx: 1` (`dir: "left"`, `dx: -9`), `idx: 2` (`dir: "left"`, `dx: -18`), `idx: 3` (`dir: "right"`, `dx: 18`) — the gap stayed at insertion slot `2` (between `10♣` and `J♦`) instead of shifting to slot `1` (between `9♦` and `10♣`).
- Telemetry (`Item 3 Step 7`): `Dragged card (6♦) not at data-index="1" (found 10♣)`.
- Visual evidence in `.reviews/screenshots/step_10_item_3_drag_part_slot_1.png`, `.reviews/screenshots/step_11_item_3_drag_drop_committed.png`, and `.reviews/screenshots/step_9_item_3_drag_part_slot_3.png` (where `mid34X = 274` also drifted `+1` slot right to slot `4` between `J♣` and `Q♣` instead of slot `3`).

## Root Cause
1. In `frontend/src/utils/handLayout.ts`, `resolveDragHandRow` recomputes `computeHandLayout({ cardCount: remainingCount, ... })` for `N - 1` cards. When `cardCount` drops from `6` to `5` (`<= HAND_SMALL_HAND_MAX_CARDS`), `computeHandLayout` re-centers the 5 remaining cards inside `defaultSixCardBoxWidth` by jumping `startX` from `0` to `32px`.
2. That `32px` shift moves `10♣` (original `x = 128`, center `178`) left to `x = 96` (center `146`) and `J♣` (original `x = 256`, center `306`) left to `x = 224` (center `274`) as soon as the drag starts.
3. `resolveDragInsertIndex` (`frontend/src/utils/handLayout.ts`) then tests `row.restingPositions[index] + row.cardWidth / 2 <= pointerX`. At the exact midpoint between original cards `1` and `2` (`146`), `146 <= 146` evaluates to `true`, jumping `insertIndex` to `2` (past `10♣`), and at `274` (`mid34X`) `274 <= 274` jumps `insertIndex` to `4` (past `J♣`).
4. `frontend/src/components/GameTable.tsx` (`compactHandLayoutState` / `baseCardPositions`) mirrors the same `N - 1` `startX` shift during drag.

## Required Changes
1. **`frontend/src/utils/handLayout.ts` (`resolveDragHandRow`, `resolveDragInsertIndex`, `computeDragPartedCardPositions`):**
   - Prevent small hands (`cardCount <= HAND_SMALL_HAND_MAX_CARDS`) from jumping their remaining cards' horizontal baseline (`startX`) during an active drag-reorder when resolving insertion slots / parting positions, OR anchor `resolveDragInsertIndex` cleanly to the non-shifted card slots and resolve exact midpoints without an off-by-1 `<=` boundary jump so dragging card `0` to the midpoint between card `1` and card `2` (`pointerX = 146`) resolves to `insertIndex = 1` (and between card `3` and card `4`, `pointerX = 274`, resolves to `insertIndex = 3`).
   - Preserve monotonic non-decreasing slot resolution across `0..boxWidth`, full-card gap opening (`>= cardWidth`) at edges and middle slots, and `0` delta outside `HAND_DRAG_PARTING_RADIUS = 3` on large hands (e.g., 14-card and 30-card hands).
2. **`frontend/src/components/GameTable.tsx` (`compactHandLayoutState`, `baseCardPositions`, `dragParting`):**
   - Keep `baseCardPositions` and `dragParting.positions` aligned on the same drag-row origin so `data-part-direction`, `data-part-delta-x`, `data-drag-insert-index`, and the rendered `translate3d(...)` / SVG hand contour match the resolved insertion slot without shifting remaining cards under a stationary pointer.
