## Requirements & Architecture

### 1. Strict Backend Authority for Throw-In Eligibility (`PlayerGameView.scala`)
Ensure `PlayerGameView.forViewer(...)` computes `canThrowIn`, `throwInCards`, and `throwInCapacity` with zero ambiguity so the frontend can strictly gate `[data-testid="throw-in-outline"]`:
- `canThrowIn` MUST be `false` (and `throwInCards` MUST be `Nil`) whenever ANY of the following hold:
  1. The viewer is the defending player (`viewerIdx == state.defenderIdx`).
  2. The viewer has already submitted a pass command in the current bout (`viewer.hasPassed == true`).
  3. The viewer has NO valid cards in hand matching any rank currently on `state.tablePairs` (or no card in `viewer.hand` produces a legal `GameAction.ThrowInCards`).
  4. The bout phase is not `BoutPhase.Defense` or `BoutPhase.SurrenderThrowIn`, `state.tablePairs.isEmpty`, or `throwInCapacity <= 0`.
- `canThrowIn` MUST be `true` if and only if the viewer can legally execute `GameAction.ThrowInCards(viewer.id, List(card))` for at least one card in `throwInCards` (i.e., `throwInCards.nonEmpty && throwInCapacity > 0 && !viewer.hasPassed && viewerIdx != state.defenderIdx`).
- Verify `throwInCapacity` accurately caps the maximum number of cards that can still be thrown in (respecting both `state.maxBoutAttacks - state.tablePairs.size` and remaining defender hand capacity / uncovered limit).

### 2. Multi-Card `ThrowInCards` Validation (`GameEngine.scala`)
- Inspect `applyThrowInCards` in `GameEngine.scala` and ensure a player can submit multiple legal throw-in cards in a single `GameAction.ThrowInCards(playerId, cards)` call (e.g., 2 or 3 cards of matching table rank(s), even across distinct ranks that are both present on `tablePairs` if all submitted cards match ranks currently on the table!).
- Enforce that:
  - `cards.nonEmpty` and all `cards` are in the acting player's hand (no duplicates exceeding hand copies).
  - Every card in `cards` matches a rank currently on `state.tablePairs`.
  - `cards.size <= remainingThrowInCapacity` (cannot exceed `maxBoutAttacks - tablePairs.size` or defender's capacity to cover/take).
  - The player is NOT the defender and has NOT already passed (`!player.hasPassed`).
  - Works identically in both `BoutPhase.Defense` and `BoutPhase.SurrenderThrowIn`.

### 3. New 1-Click QA Scenario Presets (`GameManager.scala` & `GameRoutes.scala`)
Add two deterministic, dev-friendly QA scenario presets exposed via the existing preset creation endpoint in `GameRoutes.scala` (`POST /api/games/presets/:presetId` or existing preset route in `GameManager.scala` / `GameRoutes.scala`):

1. **`throw-in-multi-qa`** ("Throw-In & Multi-Card QA"):
   - 3 human seats (`Alice` = `p0`, `Bob` = `p1`, `Charlie` = `p2`), `devMode = true`, `allowRedirect = true`.
   - Active `BoutPhase.Defense` with **all table cards covered** (e.g., `TablePair(Card(Spades, Seven), Some(Card(Spades, Eight)))`), trump = `Hearts`, `attackerIdx = 0` (Alice), `defenderIdx = 1` (Bob), `defenderInitialHandSize = 6`, talon non-empty.
   - Hands:
     - **Alice (`p0`)**: holds 3 legal throw-in cards (`7♥`, `7♦`, `7♣`) PLUS 2 non-matching cards (`K♠`, `Q♣`). Result: `canThrowIn == true`, `throwInCards` has 3 cards, `throwInCapacity >= 3`, and Alice can also `PassTurn`! Allows testing: outline visible, drag throw-in, double-click single throw-in, click-multi-select + double-click batch throw-in of 2–3 cards at once, Pass icon button, and outline disappearing immediately once Alice passes!
     - **Bob (`p1`, defender)**: holds 5 cards (e.g., `9♠`, `10♠`, `J♦`, `Q♦`, `A♣`). Result: `canThrowIn == false` -> outline MUST NOT be visible to Bob.
     - **Charlie (`p2`, non-defender attacker/helper)**: holds 4 cards with NO `7`s or `8`s (e.g., `9♣`, `10♣`, `J♠`, `A♦`). Result: `canThrowIn == false` -> outline MUST NOT be visible to Charlie.

2. **`large-hand-stress`** ("Large Hand (30+ Cards)"):
   - 2 human seats (`Alice` = `p0`, `Bob` = `p1`), `devMode = true`, `allowRedirect = true`, trump = `Hearts`.
   - Active `BoutPhase.Defense` where Bob (`p1`) attacked with an uncovered `9♣` (so `attackerIdx = 1`, `defenderIdx = 0`), talon is empty (`Vector.empty`), `bottomTrumpCard = None`.
   - **Alice (`p0`, defending active viewer)** holds **30 cards** in hand (nearly the entire 36-card deck!), containing a deliberate mix of **playable cards** (higher clubs + trumps that can cover `9♣`) and **non-playable cards** (non-trump spades/diamonds and low clubs `6♣, 7♣, 8♣`).
   - **Bob (`p1`)** holds 5 remaining cards.
   - Allows a human reviewer to verify in 1 click (<5 seconds):
     - Active hand renders 30 cards cleanly inside `[data-zone="player-hand"]` with ~20%–80% overlap, readable left-side rank & suit on every card, and zero overflow/overlap with deck or sidebars.
     - Playable cards lift upward while unplayable cards stay lower, causing the tight non-rectangular hand highlight contour to visibly distort around the lifted cards.
     - Dragging any card from the large hand keeps it visible at all times.
     - Surrender icon button is visible in the bottom-right corner for Alice.
