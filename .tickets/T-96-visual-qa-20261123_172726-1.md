# T-96-visual-qa-20261123_172726-1: Fix M2 Lobby/Multiplayer CSS Variable Scoping, Heading Contrast & Projected Table Layout Collisions

## Context & Observed Browser Visual Bugs
Real Chromium visual QA telemetry and screenshots across `LobbyPage` (`/`) and `MultiplayerGamePage` (`/g/:gameId`) uncovered multiple objective layout, alignment, and contrast failures:

1. **`ROW_VERTICAL_MISALIGNMENT` in `div[data-zone="player-hand"].mp-hand` + Arena Squeezed to 404px (`.page` & `table.css` collisions):**
   - Reported in `<browser_visual_telemetry>` at `item_2_active_projected_match` (`hand-card-5` `centerY=831` vs `hand-card-0` `centerY=735`) and `item_6_quick_pair_matched_in_progress` (`hand-card-5` `centerY=812` vs `hand-card-0` `centerY=716`), and visible in `step_3_item_2_active_projected_match.png`, `step_3_item_2_spectator_zero_leak_view.png`, and `step_7_item_6_quick_pair_matched_in_progress.png`.
   - **Root causes:**
     - `frontend/src/app.css` (`.page`, lines 172–177) caps `.multiplayer-page` to `max-width: 960px` below `1400px` (`frontend/src/styles/lobby.css` only widens `.multiplayer-page` at `@media (min-width: 1400px)`), squeezing `.mp-arena` to `404px` wide and `.mp-hand` to `375px` wide at `1280×800`. A 6-card hand (`6 × 64px` + gaps) wraps onto two rows.
     - `frontend/src/pages/MultiplayerGamePage.tsx` assigns `data-zone="table-arena"`, `data-zone="table-deck"`, and `data-zone="player-hand"` on `.mp-arena`, `.mp-deck`, and `.mp-hand`, which match global M1 selectors in `frontend/src/styles/table.css` (imported by `LobbyPage.tsx`) and `frontend/src/app.css`:
       - `table.css` `[data-zone='table-arena']` (line 114) overrides `.mp-arena`'s `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)` with `1fr 2fr 1fr`, `grid-template-rows: var(--seat-row-height) ...`, and `padding: 8px 28px`, crushing `.mp-seats-row` into the left `1fr` track (`119px` per seat, wrapping `QA Opponent 1` across 3 lines and crowding both spectator seats into the left half).
       - `app.css` `html [data-zone='table-arena']::before` (line 1682, specificity `(0,1,1)`) paints `--durak-felt` (`#dfe8dc` pale mint green under light OS `prefers-color-scheme: light`) over `.mp-arena`'s dark `#1e2923` felt, clashing with the `#161512`/`#262421` dark M2 shell.
       - `table.css` `[data-zone='table-deck']` (line 741) paints a light `#e8ecf1` panel background/border under `.mp-deck`, making `.mp-deck-count` (`color: var(--mp-text-strong)` = `#f1f1f1`, e.g. `24`) white-on-light-grey and illegible.
       - `table.css` `[data-zone='player-hand']` (line 1167) sets `grid-column: 1 / -1; grid-row: 3; display: grid; padding: 76px 0 8px;`, pushing `.mp-hand` onto a separate row below `.mp-deck` and `.mp-actions` instead of keeping `.mp-deck | .mp-hand | .mp-actions` aligned in `.mp-bottom-row`.

2. **Invisible / Unstyled `.m2-button` & `.m2-status-text` on `MultiplayerGamePage` (`--m2-*` scoped only to `.m2-lobby`):**
   - Visible in `step_5_item_4_private_waiting_room.png`, `step_6_item_5_joined_4p_public_room.png`, `step_7_item_6_quick_pair_joined_waiting.png`, `step_3_item_2_active_projected_match.png`, and `step_7_item_6_quick_pair_matched_in_progress.png`.
   - In `frontend/src/styles/lobby.css` (lines 10–21), `--m2-bg`, `--m2-surface`, `--m2-surface-raised`, `--m2-border`, `--m2-text`, `--m2-text-strong`, `--m2-muted`, `--m2-green`, `--m2-green-soft`, `--m2-red`, and `--m2-red-soft` are only defined on `.m2-lobby`, not on `.multiplayer-page`.
   - Inside `MultiplayerGamePage.tsx` (`<section class="page multiplayer-page">`), every `.m2-button` (`Copy Invite Link`, `Join Seat`, `Leave Room`, `Back to Lobby`, `Play`, `Cover`, `Redirect`, `Pass`) and `.m2-status-text` evaluates those custom properties as undefined: `Copy Invite Link` renders `#14130f` black text on transparent background over dark `#262421` (nearly invisible), and secondary/table action buttons render as floating text without borders or backgrounds.

3. **Dark-on-Dark `.m2-panel-title` Headings in `LobbyPage` (`.lobby-page h2` specificity collision):**
   - Visible in `step_1_lobby_initial.png`, `step_5_item_4_create_private_invite_form.png`, and `step_6_item_5_lobby_pool_seeded.png`.
   - `frontend/src/app.css` line 179 (`.lobby-page h2 { color: var(--app-text-strong); }`, specificity `(0,1,1)`) overrides `.m2-panel-title` (`lobby.css` line 225, specificity `(0,1,0)`). Under light OS `prefers-color-scheme: light`, `--app-text-strong` resolves to `#1e293b`, making `"Quick Pairing"`, `"Lobby Pool"`, `"Create Invite Link"`, and `"QA scenario presets"` dark slate text on `#262421` dark panels.

4. **Duplicate Selection Outline Mismatch on `.mp-hand-card.selected`:**
   - In `frontend/src/pages/MultiplayerGamePage.tsx` (lines 703–717), `.mp-hand-card.selected` (`lobby.css` line 1082) draws `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px var(--mp-green)` on the stationary `<button>`, while the inner `<PlayingCard selected={isSelected} />` (`Card.tsx` + `table.css` line 1133) applies `.card-slot.selected { transform: translateY(-8px); box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px var(--durak-accent); }`, producing two offset/mismatched selection outlines when a card is selected.

## Required Fixes
1. **Share `--m2-*` tokens across `.m2-lobby` and `.multiplayer-page` (`frontend/src/styles/lobby.css`):**
   - Declare all `--m2-*` custom properties on `.m2-lobby, .multiplayer-page` so `.m2-button`, `.m2-button.primary`, `.m2-button.secondary`, and `.m2-status-text` render with full `#629924` / `#3d3a35` / `#c33` styling and contrast inside `MultiplayerGamePage`.
   - Give `.m2-button.secondary` inside `.mp-arena` (or generally in `.multiplayer-page`) a legible surface background (`background: var(--m2-surface); border-color: var(--m2-border); color: var(--m2-text-strong);`) so `Redirect` and `Pass` read clearly as buttons over `#1e2923` felt.

2. **Fix `.m2-panel-title` specificity (`frontend/src/styles/lobby.css`):**
   - Use `.m2-lobby .m2-panel-title` (specificity `(0,2,0)`) so `color: var(--m2-text-strong); margin: 0; font-size: 1.05rem;` beats `.lobby-page h2`.

3. **Isolate `MultiplayerGamePage` from `.page`, `table.css`, and `app.css` `[data-zone=...]` overrides (`frontend/src/styles/lobby.css` & `frontend/src/pages/MultiplayerGamePage.tsx`):**
   - Override `.page`'s `max-width: 960px` on `.multiplayer-page` for all desktop viewports (`> 1100px`), e.g. `width: min(1440px, calc(100% - 2rem)); max-width: 1440px; margin-inline: auto;` (removing the restriction that only widened it at `>= 1400px`), so at `1280×800` `.mp-arena` has ample horizontal width (`~720px+`).
   - Add scoped rules in `lobby.css` (e.g. `.multiplayer-page .mp-arena[data-zone='table-arena']`, `html .multiplayer-page .mp-arena[data-zone='table-arena']::before`, `.multiplayer-page .mp-deck[data-zone='table-deck']`, `.multiplayer-page .mp-hand[data-zone='player-hand']`) that win over `table.css` and `app.css`:
     - `.mp-arena`: keep `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr); grid-template-rows: auto minmax(10rem, 1fr) auto; gap: 0.85rem; padding: 0.85rem; background: var(--mp-felt); border: 1px solid var(--mp-border); min-height: 0;` and suppress `html [data-zone='table-arena']::before` (`content: none; display: none;`).
     - `.mp-deck`: keep `display: flex; flex-direction: column; justify-content: flex-end; align-items: center; background: transparent; border: none; padding: 0;` so `.mp-deck-count` (`#f1f1f1`) sits cleanly on `#1e2923` felt with high contrast.
     - `.mp-hand`: keep `grid-column: auto; grid-row: auto; display: flex; flex-wrap: nowrap; align-items: flex-end; justify-content: center; gap: 0.35rem; padding: 0; min-height: 6rem;` on desktop (or dynamically overlap cards / tighten gap when `hand.length` is large) so a 6-card hand stays aligned on a single horizontal row (`ROW_VERTICAL_MISALIGNMENT` = 0) and `.mp-bottom-row` renders `Talon | Hand | Actions` side by side on row 3 of `.mp-arena`.
   - **CRITICAL INVARIANT:** `frontend/src/pages/LobbyPage.test.tsx` (line 331) asserts `expect(lobbyCss).not.toMatch(/position:\s*(absolute|fixed)/);`. Do NOT use `position: absolute` or `position: fixed` anywhere in `lobby.css`.

4. **Fix double selection outline on `.mp-hand-card.selected` (`frontend/src/styles/lobby.css` / `frontend/src/pages/MultiplayerGamePage.tsx`):**
   - Remove the redundant `box-shadow` on `.mp-hand-card.selected` (or move the `-8px` lift onto `.mp-hand-card.selected` and pass `selected={false}` to `<PlayingCard />`) so only one crisp selection ring conforms to the card's exact position and `border-radius`.
