# T-95-audit-gap-2: Verify & Remediate Throw-In Outline Visibility, Buttonless Multi-Card Throw-In, Icon-Only Surrender/Pass Controls, and QA Scenario Wiring

## Context
The backend (`PlayerGameView.scala`, `GameManager.scala`, `ThrowInEligibilitySuite.scala`, `PresetSuite.scala`) implements strict throw-in eligibility (`canThrowIn`, `throwInCards`, `throwInCapacity`), atomic multi-card `ThrowInCards`, and the new QA presets (`throw-in-multi-qa`, `large-hand-stress`). Because the frontend component and stylesheet diffs were truncated from the audit payload, inspect the live frontend files first (do not duplicate or overwrite working code) and fix any missing wiring, styling, interaction, or test gaps below.

## Requirements

1. **Throw-In Outline Visibility Gating (QA Playbook #1):**
   - `[data-testid="throw-in-outline"]` (across inline, surrender-above, and surrender-below placements) MUST render ONLY when the current viewer can legally throw in right now (`view.canThrowIn === true` and `view.throwInCards.length > 0` and `view.throwInCapacity > 0`, and not in bot/review mode).
   - It MUST NOT render if the viewer has no matching rank cards, is the defending player, has already passed, or if the bout has reached max capacity.

2. **Remove "Throw In" Button & Wire Drag / Multi-Select Double-Click Throw-In (New #4):**
   - Confirm no visible "Throw In" button exists anywhere in `ActionBar.tsx`, `GameTable.tsx`, or `GamePage.tsx`.
   - Throwing in a single card works by dragging a legal throw-in card onto `[data-testid="throw-in-outline"]` OR double-clicking the card.
   - Throwing in multiple cards works by clicking to toggle-select multiple legal throw-in cards (capped at `view.throwInCapacity`, ignoring illegal cards) and double-clicking to deploy all selected cards atomically via the multi-card `throwInCards` action.

3. **Themed, Smaller, Icon-Only Surrender & Pass Buttons (New #5 & New #6):**
   - Confirm `ActionBar.tsx` renders smaller, icon-only buttons for Surrender (`SurrenderIcon`) and Pass (`PassIcon`) with accessible `aria-label` / tooltip text and NO visible `"Surrender"` or `"Pass"` text labels.
   - `SurrenderIcon` must clearly depict two raised open hands beneath fanned cards with a downward arrow from cards toward the hands; `PassIcon` must share the exact same stroke weight, grid, and visual aesthetic.
   - Both buttons must remain positioned in the bottom-right corner of the game table (Row 3, Col 3) and use the dark-felt/table theme palette rather than bright red.

4. **End-to-End QA Scenario Preset Wiring (Lobby & Left Sidebar):**
   - Confirm all newly added and relevant QA presets (`throw-in-multi-qa`, `large-hand-stress`, `throw-in-visibility-4p`, `throw-in-capacity-cap`, `huge-hand-36`, `huge-hand-52`) are wired cleanly in both `LobbyPage.tsx` and the `GamePage.tsx` left-sidebar `⚡ QA Scenarios` panel so a human reviewer can reach every verification state in 1 click.
