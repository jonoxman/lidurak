# T-96-visual-qa-20261124_140836-1: Fix Quick Pairing Queue Stay-on-Lobby, Tall Tiles, "Create a Game" Reorder, and M1 Theme Styling

## Summary
Implemented feedback items **QA #6**, **New #1**, and **New #2** failed to render in the live Chromium screenshots (`step_1`–`step_10`). Players clicking Quick Pairing are immediately placed into a new room and navigated to `/g/:gameId`, Quick Pairing tiles remain short, the third lobby tab is still named `"Create Invite Link"` with `Visibility` last, and the M2 lobby/waiting room ignores Milestone 1 design tokens and Profile Theme switching (`Light` / `Dark` / `Device theme`).

## Exact Requirements & Root Causes

### 1. QA #6 — Keep Quick Pairing players on the lobby until matched
- **Observed bug:** In `step_2_item_6_quick_pair_searching_stays_on_lob.png`, clicking `2P • 15s` navigates immediately to `/g/fc7dxWpK` (`Room fc7dxWpK` waiting screen).
- **Root cause:**
  - `backend/src/main/scala/durak/lobby/QuickPairingService.scala` (`pairLocked`, lines 84–107) immediately calls `roomManager.createRoom(session, config)` and returns `(room.gameId, seatIndex)` even when the player is still waiting for opponents.
  - `frontend/src/components/lobby/QuickPairingTab.tsx` (`start`, lines 39–54) immediately calls `onMatched(matched.gameId)` on the `joinQuickPair(preset)` response, navigating off `/` into `WaitingRoom`.
- **Required fix:**
  - In `backend/src/main/scala/durak/lobby/QuickPairingService.scala` and `backend/src/main/scala/durak/http/MultiplayerRoutes.scala` (plus `frontend/src/api/lobbyClient.ts` / `frontend/src/components/lobby/QuickPairingTab.tsx`):
    - Hold queued players in a Quick Pairing queue (not seated inside an active/waiting room in `RoomManager` or listed as an open Lobby Pool room) until `playerCount` matching players are available to form a live match.
    - Keep the searching user on `/` with `[data-testid="quick-pair-searching"]` visible and Cancel functional (`DELETE /api/quick-pair`).
    - Only navigate to `/g/:gameId` (`onMatched(gameId)`) when a match is actually found (e.g., via `WsServerMessage.MatchFound(gameId)` over `/ws/lobby` or when the matching request completes a full match). Keep the lobby WebSocket (`/ws/lobby`) active whenever the lobby is mounted (not only after opening the `Lobby Pool` tab) so `onMatchFound` is always delivered while searching on the Quick Pairing tab.

### 2. New #1 — Double Quick Pairing tile height & rename/reorder "Create a Game"
- **Observed bug:** In `step_4_item_1_lobby_quick_pair_tall_tiles.png` and `step_5_item_1_create_a_game_reordered_controls.png`, `.quick-pair-tile` is ~64px tall (`padding: 0.85rem 0.75rem`), the third tab is still `"Create Invite Link"`, and `Visibility` is still last.
- **Required fix:**
  - `frontend/src/styles/lobby.css` (`.quick-pair-tile`): Roughly double the tile height (e.g., `min-height: 8rem` / ~128px with centered/balanced vertical spacing) while keeping the existing 3-column desktop / 2-column mobile grid layout and text structure.
  - `frontend/src/pages/LobbyPage.tsx` (`LOBBY_TABS` line 41): Rename `{ id: 'invite', label: 'Create Invite Link' }` to `'Create a Game'`.
  - `frontend/src/components/lobby/CreateInviteTab.tsx`:
    - Rename `aria-label` and `<h2 class="m2-panel-title">` from `"Create Invite Link"` to `"Create a Game"`.
    - Reorder the form controls so the **Visibility** choice (`Private invite link` / `Public lobby pool` — whether the game is a private link or not) is **first**, followed by `Players`, `Redirect / reflect`, and `Time control per turn` in their existing order.

### 3. New #2 — Adopt Milestone 1 visual design & Profile Theme responsiveness
- **Observed bug:** In `step_1_lobby_initial.png`, `step_7_item_2_lobby_dark_theme_create_game.png`, `step_9_item_2_lobby_light_theme_create_game.png`, and `step_10_item_2_lobby_light_theme_searching.png`, `.m2-lobby` and `.multiplayer-page` hardcode dark `--m2-*` and `--mp-*` colors (`#161512`, `#262421`, `#629924`) and fixed `12px`/`10px` border radii, ignoring `html[data-theme='light']` and `--app-roundness`.
- **Required fix:**
  - Update `frontend/src/styles/lobby.css` (and any inline/component classes in `LobbyPage.tsx`, `QuickPairingTab.tsx`, `LobbyPoolTab.tsx`, `CreateInviteTab.tsx`, `QaLobbyPresets.tsx`, `MultiplayerGamePage.tsx` waiting room) so all lobby and waiting-room surfaces, borders, text, inputs, tabs, segmented controls, badges, and buttons derive from Milestone 1's CSS custom properties in `frontend/src/app.css` (`--app-bg`, `--app-panel`, `--bg-surface`, `--bg-elevated`, `--app-border`, `--app-text`, `--app-text-strong`, `--app-roundness`, `--ui-select-accent`, `--ui-select-border`, `--ui-select-bg`, `--ui-select-glow`, `--ui-select-text`, `--app-accent`), with proper contrast and styling in both `Dark` and `Light` (and `Device theme`) modes.
